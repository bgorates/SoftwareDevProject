Employee Scheduling & Shift Management System

Project Description:
This system automates employee shift scheduling for a company based on employee availability, workload requirements, and manager rules. Employees can submit availability, request shift swaps or time off, and check approval status, while managers can generate optimized schedules, approve/reject requests, and manage overall workforce allocation. The system aims to reduce scheduling conflicts, improve employee satisfaction, and save managers time.

⸻

Functional Features
	1.	Employee Features
	•	Submit availability for shifts (weekly/monthly).
	•	Request time off or shift swaps.
	•	View assigned shifts and schedule.
	•	Receive notifications when requests are approved/rejected.
	•	Provide feedback or preferences for certain shifts.
	2.	Manager Features
	•	Generate schedules automatically based on employee availability, skill requirements, and company policies.
	•	Manually adjust schedules when needed.
	•	Approve or reject employee requests (time off, shift swaps).
	•	Track employee hours and attendance.
	•	Generate reports: weekly/monthly schedules, employee workload, unfilled shifts, and overtime hours.
	3.	System Features
	•	Conflict detection: alert when shifts overlap or staffing is insufficient.
	•	Notifications via email/app for both managers and employees.
	•	Audit log of schedule changes and approvals.
	•	User authentication and role-based access (employee, manager, admin).
	•	Export schedules to PDF/Excel for offline use.


 Non-Functional Features
	•	Performance: Handle large numbers of employees and shifts without lag, especially during schedule generation.
	•	Scalability: Support small to large companies with hundreds of employees.
	•	Security: Role-based access control; encrypt sensitive employee data.
	•	Reliability: Ensure schedules are consistent; prevent double-booking or missed shifts.
	•	Availability: High uptime during critical scheduling periods.
	•	Maintainability: Easy to update rules, add new roles, and manage employees.
	•	Compliance: Ensure legal working hours and labor laws are considered in schedule generation.
